**Subject**: Question about the terms of use of the KLiCKe corpus for a secondary analysis

Dear Dr. Tian,

I am [name], an independent researcher at [affiliation]. Thank you for making the KLiCKe corpus publicly available; its size and the pairing of keystroke logs with final texts make it a valuable resource.

I am planning a secondary analysis of the corpus that examines how much information about the final text remains in keystroke logs after common de-identification steps applied before public release (for example, replacing alphanumeric characters with a placeholder, reducing keys to categories, or rounding timestamps). The aim is to give guidance to researchers who release keystroke data. The analysis would use only the keystroke logs and the essays; I would not use the demographic file, would not attempt to identify any participant, and would not redistribute the data.

Before I start, I would be grateful if you could help me with three questions:

1. **Terms of use.** I understand from the Inputlog community page that the files "can be downloaded and used freely" provided that the repository is referenced as the source. Could you confirm that these are the terms for the data (the GitHub repository shows an MIT License, and the article is published under CC BY-NC-ND 3.0), and that the use described above is within them?
2. **Consent and ethics.** Did the consent form shown to participants mention that the data would be shared publicly and could be used in secondary research? If an ethics board reviewed the data collection, could you share the name of the board (and the protocol number, if it can be shared)? I would like to describe this accurately in the ethics section of the paper.
3. **Acceptable use.** Do you see any concern with an analysis of this kind, which estimates how easily the text could be matched from processed logs? If you would prefer that I report the results in a particular way (for example, only in aggregate), I will follow that.

I will cite the corpus and your article in any publication. Thank you very much for your time.
