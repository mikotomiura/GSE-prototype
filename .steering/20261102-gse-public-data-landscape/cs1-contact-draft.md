Subject: Request for access to the CS1 keystroke datasets (doi:10.7910/DVN/6BPCXN)

Dear Dr. Edwards,

I am Mikoto Miura, an independent researcher (ORCID 0009-0000-4196-0508). I study how the timing of keystrokes and pauses during ordinary desk work can indicate when a person's work is stalling, without storing what they type.

I would like to use the 2019 CS1 keystroke dataset (doi:10.7910/DVN/6BPCXN) described in your JEDM 2023 paper. The termsOfAccess asks researchers to contact you before using the files, so I am writing first.

How I plan to use it:

- Only the event timestamps and the type of each event (insert, delete, cursor movement, run). I would map each event to a coarse key category and discard the inserted and deleted text without storing it.
- Descriptive summaries only: the distribution of pauses, how often long stops in text production occur, and how these vary within and between students. No attempt to identify or re-identify anyone, and no linking with other data.
- I would not download or use the assignment descriptions, the survey file, or students.csv.
- The raw files would stay on my own computer and would not be redistributed. Only aggregate numbers would appear in publications or public repositories, with your dataset cited.

If this use is acceptable, I would be grateful for your permission. If there are conditions you would like me to follow, please let me know.

Best regards,
Mikoto Miura
