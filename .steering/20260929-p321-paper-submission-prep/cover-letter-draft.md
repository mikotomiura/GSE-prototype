Dear Editors,

I am submitting the manuscript "When 'no help' is not 'solved': outcome-agnostic self-resolution measures in two public debugging datasets" for consideration as a research paper in *Empirical Software Engineering*.

Observational studies of debugging report how often developers consult documentation, the web or other people, and the complement of such figures is easily read as problems resolved without help. That reading holds only if every unit without consultation ends in a fix. In the two public, human-coded datasets of professional debugging that I reanalyse, a unit also ends when the developer stops or when observation ends. The manuscript defines an outcome-agnostic and an outcome-requiring measure, decomposes their difference exactly, and estimates it under several operational definitions of consultation and resolution. It also reports that the consultation figures of one of the original studies could not all be reproduced from its shared coding under any of the four definitions examined, and it recommends how such measures should be reported.

The study is a secondary analysis of openly licensed data and publications (CC BY 4.0); no data were collected from human participants. One of the two datasets comes from a study published in this journal (Alaboudi and LaToza 2023), which is one reason I consider the journal a suitable venue. The manuscript describes the reproduction attempt factually and does not attribute the differences to any particular cause.

The analysis code, the analysis configuration (first committed before the analysis code and the results, with later changes logged; the manuscript discloses which estimates were known earlier), the transcribed data and all results are available in a replication package archived at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23029603. Once the coded data of the first dataset have been downloaded from the original replication packages, the package reproduces every result reported in the manuscript. The use of an LLM-based assistant is described in Sect. 5.5 of the manuscript.

The manuscript has not been published and is not under consideration elsewhere. [It has not been posted as a preprint. I intend to post it on arXiv under a CC BY 4.0 licence during the review and will inform the editorial office of its identifier.] I have no competing interests to declare.
