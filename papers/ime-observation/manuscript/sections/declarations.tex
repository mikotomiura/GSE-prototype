\section*{Declarations}

\bmhead{Funding}
This research received no specific grant from any funding agency in the public, commercial or not-for-profit sectors.

\bmhead{Conflicts of interest/Competing interests}
The author has no competing interests to declare that are relevant to the content of this article.

\bmhead{Ethics approval}
Not applicable: no participants were recruited, and all input of the measurement was generated by a program. The logs
were checked for key presses that the program did not send, by the stopping rule right after each run and again at
deposit; the blind spots of these checks are stated in Section~\ref{sec:limitations}.

\bmhead{Consent to participate}
Not applicable.

\bmhead{Consent for publication}
Not applicable.

\bmhead{Availability of data and materials}
The dataset described in Section~\ref{sec:dataset}, with its datasheet and the specification of its formats, is
deposited in Zenodo under the persistent identifier \tbd{DOI of the dataset}. The two registrations are on OSF
Registries \citep{prereg-macos,prereg-windows}. The plans, the procedures, the phrase corpus, the settings of the
generator and the seed are part of the dataset and of the code repository.

\bmhead{Code availability}
The logger, the kit that generates the plans, runs the cells and checks the logs, the analysis code, and the script that
generates the tables, the values quoted in the text and Figures 2 to 5 of this article from the result files are
released in a public repository, \tbd{URL of the repository}, with an archived release under \tbd{DOI of the release}.
The analyses reported as registered were run from the freezing tags of the registration (Table~\ref{tab:freezes}).

\bmhead{AI declaration}
The author used two tools based on large language models. Claude (Anthropic; Opus, Sonnet and Haiku models, used
through the Claude Code command-line tool) was used to write and revise the code of the logger and of the kit (the plan
generator, the runner, the reference input fields and the analysis), the script that generates the tables, the values
quoted in the text and Figures 2 to 5 from the result files, and the code of Figure 1; to search for and summarise the
documentation and the published work examined; to draft sections of this article from the author's outline, the
registered protocol and the generated values; and to review the code and the text. Codex (OpenAI, used through its
command-line tool) was used to review the code. The research questions, the design, the registered protocol and its
deviations, the measurements, and the interpretation of the results were decided by the author. No AI tool produced
data: every result value comes from the measurements through the registered analysis and is copied into the text by
the script. The author reviewed the code with its tests and the text against the registration, the records and the
result files, and is accountable for the content of this article.

\bmhead{Authors' contributions}
Mikoto Miura is the sole author: conceptualization, methodology, software, investigation, formal analysis, data
curation, visualization, and writing (original draft, review and editing).

\section*{Open Practices Statement}

The study was pre-registered on OSF Registries in two parts, for the macOS cells (\url{https://osf.io/9nh7r},
DOI 10.17605/OSF.IO/9NH7R) and, in an addendum frozen before the first Windows measurement, for the Windows cell
(\url{https://osf.io/4jwzn}, DOI 10.17605/OSF.IO/4JWZN). The code, the materials and the analysis scripts are available
in a public repository with an archived release (\tbd{DOI of the release}), and the data are available under their own
persistent identifier (\tbd{DOI of the dataset}). The analyses reported as registered were run from the freezing tags;
the deviations from the registrations are listed in Table~\ref{tab:deviations}. Not registered are the analysis of
observability (Section~\ref{sec:observability}), the descriptive statements about what can be computed from the logs
(Table~\ref{tab:measures}) and about the uses that the records support (Section~\ref{sec:dataset-uses}), the
recommendations of Section~\ref{sec:recommendations} as recommendations, the rule by which the abstract and the
conclusions select their estimates beyond section~6.1 of the registration, and the analyses labelled as exploratory.
The outline that fixed the rules for reporting before the results were analysed is in the public repository as commit
\tbd{commit of the outline}.
