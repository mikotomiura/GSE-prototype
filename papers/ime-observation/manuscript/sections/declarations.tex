\bmhead{Ethics approval}
Not applicable: no participants were recruited, and all input of the measurement was generated by a program. The logs
were checked for key presses that the program did not send, by the stopping rule right after each run and again at
deposit; the blind spots of these checks are stated in Section~\ref{sec:limitations}.

\section*{Open Practices Statement}

The study was pre-registered on OSF Registries in two parts, for the macOS cells (\url{https://osf.io/9nh7r},
DOI 10.17605/OSF.IO/9NH7R) and, in an addendum frozen before the first Windows measurement, for the Windows cell
(\url{https://osf.io/4jwzn}, DOI 10.17605/OSF.IO/4JWZN). The code, the materials and the analysis scripts are available
in a public repository with an archived release (\tbd{DOI of the release}), and the data are available under their own
persistent identifier (\tbd{DOI of the dataset}). The analyses reported as registered were run from the freezing tags;
the deviations from the registrations are listed in Table~\ref{tab:deviations}. Not registered are the analysis of
observability (Section~\ref{sec:observability}), the descriptive statements about what can be computed from the logs
(Table~\ref{tab:measures}) and about the uses that the records support (Section~\ref{sec:dataset-uses}), the
recommendations of Section~\ref{sec:recommendations} as recommendations, the rule by which the abstract and the
conclusions select their estimates beyond section~6.1 of the registration, and the analyses labelled as exploratory.
The outline that fixed the rules for reporting before the results were analysed is in the public repository as commit
\tbd{commit of the outline}.
