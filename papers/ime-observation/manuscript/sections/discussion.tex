\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Where the disagreements arise on macOS.} The rows of the macOS logger followed its own rules in almost every
session. In the primary scope they agreed with the rows that the rules specify for the presses sent with pooled recall
\rv{macos-standard-live-off.pooled.predobs.underlimit.recall} with live conversion off and
\rv{macos-standard-live-on.pooled.predobs.underlimit.recall} with it on, and the limits of agreement of their durations
spanned \rv{macos-standard-live-off.predobs.primary.dwell.loa.span} and
\rv{macos-standard-live-on.predobs.primary.dwell.loa.span} over the speeds, with median absolute differences of
\rv{macos-standard-live-off.predobs.primary.dwell.mae.span} and
\rv{macos-standard-live-on.predobs.primary.dwell.mae.span}.
The comparison of the reference with the specified rows, which involves no observation, gave the same picture as the
comparison with the logger: pooled recall \rv{macos-standard-live-off.pooled.gtpred.underlimit.recall} and
\rv{macos-standard-live-on.pooled.gtpred.underlimit.recall}, against
\rv{macos-standard-live-off.pooled.gtobs.underlimit.recall} and
\rv{macos-standard-live-on.pooled.gtobs.underlimit.recall}, the same lowest strata, and durations whose limits of
agreement spanned \rv{macos-standard-live-off.gtpred.primary.dwell.loa.span} and
\rv{macos-standard-live-on.gtpred.primary.dwell.loa.span}, with median absolute differences of
\rv{macos-standard-live-off.gtpred.primary.dwell.mae.span} and \rv{macos-standard-live-on.gtpred.primary.dwell.mae.span}
(against \rv{macos-standard-live-off.gtobs.primary.dwell.loa.span} and
\rv{macos-standard-live-on.gtobs.primary.dwell.loa.span}, and \rv{macos-standard-live-off.gtobs.primary.dwell.mae.span}
and \rv{macos-standard-live-on.gtobs.primary.dwell.mae.span}, for the logger). The disagreements of the macOS logger,
and the long tails of its duration differences, thus arise mainly where the sessions that its rules define differ from
the sessions of the reference; against its own rules the logger's miss rate was
\rv{macos-standard-live-off.pooled.predobs.underlimit.missrate} and
\rv{macos-standard-live-on.pooled.predobs.underlimit.missrate}, and its false-positive rate
\rv{macos-standard-live-off.pooled.predobs.underlimit.falseposrate} and
\rv{macos-standard-live-on.pooled.predobs.underlimit.falseposrate}. The three comparisons are read side by side and are
not combined arithmetically (Section~\ref{sec:method-analysis}).

\paragraph{Disagreements that the registration listed and those it did not.} The registration listed, as following from
the rules, a session that Backspace keeps open and carries into the next trial (S6), a session closed by Tab when a
predictive candidate is chosen (S10), a reading split by the minus, comma or period key, and, with live conversion on, a
session closed at the first of two Returns or Escapes. These set the lowest strata of the primary scope, S6 and S10 at
the fast speed (Section~\ref{sec:results-detection}). The prediction marks the first and the third as layers (a session
carried into the next trial, a closing key inside a reading), together with the trials that receive a carried session,
and these layers hold its lowest agreement (supplement). In the
trials outside every layer that the prediction marks, recall was
\rv{macos-standard-live-off.layer.none.gtobs.primary.recall} and precision
\rv{macos-standard-live-off.layer.none.gtobs.primary.precision} with live conversion off, and
\rv{macos-standard-live-on.layer.none.gtobs.primary.recall} and
\rv{macos-standard-live-on.layer.none.gtobs.primary.precision} with it on, and the specified rows agreed with the
reference there to the same extent (recall \rv{macos-standard-live-off.layer.none.gtpred.primary.recall} and
\rv{macos-standard-live-on.layer.none.gtpred.primary.recall}). The layers do not mark the sessions closed by Tab in S10
or, with live conversion on, those closed at the first of two Returns or Escapes, so disagreements that the
registration listed remain among these trials, and this study did not separate them from any that it did not list.

\paragraph{The spread of the durations on macOS.} For the sessions that paired, the spread is not explained by the
timing of the logger's records:
its key rows followed the injected presses by a median of \rv{macos-standard-live-off.kt.obs.delay.all.median} (95th
percentile \rv{macos-standard-live-off.kt.obs.delay.all.p95}) with live conversion off and
\rv{macos-standard-live-on.kt.obs.delay.all.median} (\rv{macos-standard-live-on.kt.obs.delay.all.p95}) with it on, and
its durations matched those of its rules to within the few milliseconds given above. Nor does it reflect how people
type, because the input was produced by a program. The reference's sessions begin and end with the calls that the input
method makes to the field, which the rules approximate from the keys. Differences in where the two place the beginning
and the end of a session are consistent with the spread: for example a session that the rules close at a key while the
input method keeps the reading, or, with live conversion on, the commits that the input method makes while the reading
is typed, which would also be consistent with the larger median absolute differences with live conversion on than off.
These mechanisms were not tested. The beginnings and the ends of the sessions were not compared separately; the two
macOS cells used different plans (\rv{macos-standard-live-off.pred.trials.n} and
\rv{macos-standard-live-on.pred.trials.n} trials), so the two settings are not compared trial by trial; durations are
compared only for the sessions that paired, which differ between strata and speeds; and the delay between an injected
press and the input method's calls to the reference field was not measured. The delays above cover the path from the
injection to the row; with a physical keyboard, such as a USB keyboard sampled every 8~ms \citep{pinet2017}, the delay
from a key to a row is expected to be longer (Section~\ref{sec:limitations}).

\paragraph{Windows.} The Windows logger records the visibility of the input method's windows, not the composition, so
its agreement includes the difference between the two constructs (Section~\ref{sec:observability-consequence}). Its
durations were shorter than the reference's at every speed (intervals of the bias spanning
\rv{windows-msime-tsf.primary.dwellbias.range}, median absolute differences
\rv{windows-msime-tsf.gtobs.primary.dwell.mae.span}), and recall fell with speed also in S1, in which few sessions were
split (supplement). A window that appears after the composition begins or disappears before it ends is consistent with
shorter rows; a change of the window that the logger does not observe, and, on the cloaking path, an observed interval
that ends at least 150~ms before the row's time, for which the matching is not adjusted, are consistent with sessions
that fail to pair. Which of them acted cannot be told here, because a row does not record its path and
nothing outside the software observed the windows. The values of the two systems describe different constructs and are
not compared; each is read against the construct that its logger records.

\paragraph{The definition of a Windows session.} The definition of the analysis, which keeps the sessions in which the
field reported a candidate element, left out \rv{windows-msime-tsf.defs.primary.leftout} of the
\rv{windows-msime-tsf.defs.primary.common.sessions} reference sessions of the primary scope (deviation D12). Under the
definition common to both systems, the ranges of the per-stratum intervals that the conclusions quote were
\rv{windows-msime-tsf.defs.primary.common.recall.range} for recall and
\rv{windows-msime-tsf.defs.primary.common.precision.range} for precision, against
\rv{windows-msime-tsf.primary.recall.range} and \rv{windows-msime-tsf.primary.precision.range} under the definition of
the analysis. Within these ranges, the recall of \rv{windows-msime-tsf.defs.primary.change.recall.strata} of the
\rv{windows-msime-tsf.defs.primary.change.recall.of} strata that enter both changed with the definition, by at most
\rv{windows-msime-tsf.defs.primary.change.recall.max}, and the precision of
\rv{windows-msime-tsf.defs.primary.change.precision.strata} of \rv{windows-msime-tsf.defs.primary.change.precision.of},
by at most
\rv{windows-msime-tsf.defs.primary.change.precision.max}; the pooled merge rate was
\rv{windows-msime-tsf.defs.primary.common.mergerate} under the common definition and
\rv{windows-msime-tsf.pooled.gtobs.underlimit.mergerate} under the definition of the analysis.
