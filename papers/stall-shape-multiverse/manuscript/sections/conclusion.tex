\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Across 89 definitions of a window-based stall label and 18 of a fixed-threshold pause label, the association between
the label rate and essay score changed direction within each detector: for stalls only where a 15-s window met a writer
quantile, and for pauses between mostly negligible values. Adjusting for essay length left no stall definition negative
and most values negligible. Whether stall counts depart from a constant rate within writers depended on the definition
in CS1, a rate ratio between two editions of a programming course reversed for a fifth of the definitions, below a
criterion set after seeing point values, and the largest component of CS1 stall length stayed. A study that relates a
pause or stall rate to an outcome, or compares rates between datasets, can therefore not rest on one definition: it
should report the range of its conclusion across definitions and adjustments, with intervals, and say whether that range
reads as consistent, negligible or dependent on the definition.
