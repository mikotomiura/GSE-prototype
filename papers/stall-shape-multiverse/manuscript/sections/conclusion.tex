\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Across 89 definitions of a window-based stall label and 18 of a fixed-threshold pause label, the association between
the label rate and essay score was present for most definitions, but its direction changed within each detector: for
stalls only with a 15-s window, and for pauses from negligible positive values at 2~s to small negative ones at 10~s.
Adjusting for essay length left no definition negative. Whether stall counts depart from a constant rate within
writers also depended on the definition, and a rate ratio between two editions of a programming course reversed for a
fifth of the definitions. Reports of pause and stall rates should give the range of the conclusion across definitions
and adjustments, with intervals. Every result is also reported for two segmentations that need only the time of each key
press and whether it inserted or deleted a character; the default observation intervals depend on the companion
study's estimator.
