\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Across 89 defensible definitions of a keystroke stall, the strength of the association between the stall rate and
essay score moved from negligible to small-to-moderate and turned weakly positive in the corner with the shortest
window. The rank and ratio of rates between a programming course and an essay corpus, and whether counts are
overdispersed within writers, also moved; the dominance of stall-to-stall variation in stall length in CS1 stayed.
Reports of stall labels should give the range of the conclusion across definitions, with intervals, and the released
procedure produces it for any log with key-press times.
