\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Across 89 definitions of a window-based stall label and 36 of an interval-threshold pause label, the direction of the
association between the label rate and essay score changed within each detector, for stalls only with a 15-s window
and for pauses between the 2-s and 10-s thresholds, and adjusting for typing speed or essay length moved it as much as
the definition did. The ratio of rates between a programming course and an essay corpus, and whether counts depart
from a constant rate within writers, also moved; the dominance of stall-to-stall variation in stall length in CS1 stayed.
Reports of pause and stall labels should give the range of the conclusion across definitions and adjustments, with
intervals. The released procedure needs only the time of each key press and whether it inserted or deleted a
character.
