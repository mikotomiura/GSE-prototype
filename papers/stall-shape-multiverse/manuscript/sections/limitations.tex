\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{No ground truth.} The data have no self-reports or think-aloud, so we cannot say which definition is right.
A stall is a pause in character production, not a judgement that the writer was stuck.

\paragraph{The range varied.} All proportions are of the 89 definitions examined. The grid is centred on the detector's
default; the 15-s window was set at half the default, and we did not find a prior use of it. Fixed thresholds make up
four in ten definitions. We did not vary thresholds proportional to within-word speed \cite{guo2020scenario} or
definitions that split pauses by linguistic location, because we fixed a grid that applies alike to code and essays. We
did not compare groups within a dataset, and the agreement in time between labels is not corrected for chance.

\paragraph{Differences between datasets.} The datasets differ at once in the task, the recording (CS1 key presses are
reconstructed from edit events) and the length of units, so differences between them are not attributed to a cause.

\paragraph{Counts and lengths.} The levels of variation in counts could not be separated (S6). The dispersion of
assignments includes differences between assignments. A $\varphi$ near 1 does not establish a Poisson process: the
window of the detector can make counts underdispersed while heterogeneity makes them overdispersed. The length
decomposition excludes stalls that did not resume (S5), and CS1 days are inferred from gaps of 6~h.

\paragraph{Analyses added after results.} Five computations were added after the primary results
(Section~\ref{sec:status}, S1).

\paragraph{One outcome, one measure of speed, no causal claim.} The score association rests on one holistic score in
KLiCKe and was not checked against a second outcome (the CS1 2021 release has grades, but only for 31 seen writers). The
speed measure is a median of short intervals; for writers whose quantile $\theta$ is below 2~s it can include some of
the intervals that start a stall, and short intervals of deletion inside stalls (we also measured speed on intervals
between character presses only). Speed was adjusted linearly on ranks. We do not say whether speed confounds or
mediates the association. We did not examine how the stall rate relates to the length of observation or of the essay,
and we say nothing about Japanese input, input method editors, natural work or other populations.
