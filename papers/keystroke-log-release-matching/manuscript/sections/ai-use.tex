An AI assistant (Claude, Anthropic) was used throughout this work. It drafted the design proposals, the text of the
preregistration and the predictions, from which the author chose; a log of decisions records the questions on which

search for related work and for pre-registration and pre-submission review; the points they raised were checked
against primary sources before the author decided whether to adopt them, and these reviews are not evidence for any
claim of the paper. No keystroke log or text of a participant was given to an AI system. The author has checked and
is responsible for the accuracy, originality and integrity of the output of all AI-based tools.
