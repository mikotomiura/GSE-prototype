\begin{ai}
An AI assistant (Claude, Anthropic) was used throughout this work. It drafted the design proposals, the text of the
preregistration and the predictions, from which the authors chose; a log of decisions records the questions on which
the authors chose an option other than the one the assistant recommended. It wrote and reviewed the code for the
processing, the attackers and the aggregation, and it drafted the text of this paper. AI-based tools were also used to
search for related work and for pre-registration and pre-submission review; the points they raised were checked
against primary sources before the authors decided whether to adopt them, and these reviews are not evidence for any
claim of the paper. No keystroke log or text of a participant was given to an AI system: the assistant worked from the
output of the scripts, which are written to report counts and aggregates and to print no writer identifier, no
text and no log (\S\ref{sec:data:checks}). The authors have checked and are responsible for the accuracy, originality and integrity of the output of all AI-based tools.
\end{ai}
