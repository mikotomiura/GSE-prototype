\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We asked how far each form of processing keeps a keystroke log apart from its text against a party that holds
candidate texts, and what each form costs the process measures of writing research. Against attackers that know the
processing, the masking used in one public release left every essay matched among 1,000 candidates, and only segment
values coarsened to four or more, which compute no process measure, brought these attackers with their main penalty
within four times chance; stronger attackers tried afterwards as an exploration did not stay there. In that release, hiding the
characters did not keep the logs apart from texts held elsewhere. These are measurements of the attackers and corpora
tried, not bounds on the risk.
