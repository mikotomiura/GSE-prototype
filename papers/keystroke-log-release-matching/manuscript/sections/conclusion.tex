\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We asked how far each form of processing keeps a keystroke log apart from its text against a party that holds
candidate texts, and what each form costs the process measures of writing research. Against attackers that know the
processing, the masking used in one public release left every essay matched among 1,000 candidates, and no form
evaluated kept both matching low and the process measures close to their unprocessed values. For releases whose texts
are held elsewhere, hiding the characters did not keep the logs apart from their texts. These are measurements of the
attackers and corpora tried, not bounds on the risk.
