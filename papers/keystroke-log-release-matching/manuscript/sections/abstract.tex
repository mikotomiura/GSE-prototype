% The abstract, shared by main.tex (the submission) and preprint.tex (the preprint).
  Keystroke logs record how a text was written. One public release of essay logs masked every letter and digit, with
  the stated goal of preventing reproduction of the essays, while the essays themselves had been collected and scored
  and existed outside the release. A log linked to its text tells the holder of the text how its writer wrote it, with the
  pauses, revisions and any score; in a classroom this would concern a named student. We ask how far each form of
  processing keeps a keystroke log apart from its text, which is how we read that goal, against a party that holds
  candidate texts, and what each form costs the process measures of writing research. In a preregistered evaluation on
  500 writers sampled from a public corpus of 4,992 essays with their logs, attackers that know the processing chose
  the true text among $N$ candidates on the same prompt. Against the masking, an attacker that rebuilds the final
  draft chose the true essay among 1,000 for every log. Keeping only the categories of the pressed keys and their press
  times still left 86.7\% at $N = 1{,}000$. In the main analysis, of the forms that no other form dominated in both matching and the measures, suppressing a
  quarter of the presses left 14.3\% with a median correlation of 0.81 with the unprocessed measures (51.2\% with the
  registered lighter penalty, computed after the main results), and suppressing half left 2.2\% with 0.70, where the total length alone was the
  strongest attacker; only forms that compute no measure came closer to chance. Attackers tried after these results,
  as an exploration, raised the quarter suppression to 62.3\% and segment values coarsened to four to 48.2\%, but
  not the half suppression. On Japanese translation logs typed with an input method, matching without any string chose the true translation
  in 51.6\% of logs among 12 or 13 candidates, against 7.9\% by chance and 11.9\% by the length. The results describe
  the attackers tried and are not bounds on the risk.
